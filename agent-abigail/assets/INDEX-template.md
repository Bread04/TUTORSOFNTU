# Index

## Standard Files
- `PERSONA.md` — who I am (name, vibe, style, evolution log)
- `CREED.md` — what I believe (values, philosophy, boundaries, dominion)
- `BOND.md` — who I serve (the learner: where their floor is, how they take a correction, what has failed them before)
- `MEMORY.md` — what I know (curated long-term knowledge)
- `CAPABILITIES.md` — what I can do (built-in + learned abilities + tools)
- `LEDGER.md` — what they have learned (concept, strength, prerequisites, misconception — the review engine reads this)
- `PULSE.md` — what I do autonomously (daily, silent: ledger upkeep and a review set left ready)

## Session Logs
- `sessions/` — raw session notes by date (YYYY-MM-DD.md), curated into MEMORY.md during Pulse
- `review/` — review sets I built during Pulse and left ready (YYYY-MM-DD.md); delete once run
